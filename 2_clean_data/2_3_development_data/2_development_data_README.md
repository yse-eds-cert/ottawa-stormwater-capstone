# Cleaned Development Data

This folder will contain structured datasets extracted from development plans and stormwater management reports for the selected Ottawa case study.

Data preprocessing will include:

- Extracting and standardizing site measurements.
- Recording total site area and building footprints.
- Identifying paved and planted surface areas.
- Standardizing area measurements in square metres.
- Documenting runoff coefficients and their sources.
- Checking for missing or inconsistent values.

The processed data will establish baseline site conditions for the stormwater runoff analysis.

Alternative layouts and plausible runoff coefficients for planted areas will be tested to evaluate the feasibility of replacing paved surfaces with planted space.

Original documents will remain unchanged in `1_raw_data/1_3_development_data/`.
