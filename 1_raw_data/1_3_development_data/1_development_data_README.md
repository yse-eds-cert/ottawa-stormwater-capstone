
# Ottawa Development Data

This folder will contain original development plans and stormwater management reports for the selected Ottawa case study.

**Source:** https://devapps.ottawa.ca/

The documents will be reviewed to extract:

- Total site area
- Building footprints and roof areas
- Paved surface areas
- Planted surface areas
- Runoff coefficients
- Relevant stormwater management assumptions

These measurements will support comparisons of existing site conditions and simulated alternative layouts.

Several plausible runoff coefficients for planted areas will be tested to evaluate their influence on stormwater runoff and design feasibility.

Original documents will be preserved without modification.
